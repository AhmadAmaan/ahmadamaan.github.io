01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A little curiosity.
A lot of side quests.

I graduated from Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur with a major in Mechanical Engineering and minors in Computer Systems and Machine Learning. My interest in programming started in school, where I chose Computer Studies in both ICSE and ISC. That interest followed me to college: all my open electives were CSE courses, earning me the nickname “pseudo CSE guy” among my department batchmates.

I’ve been at Oracle since graduating and am currently a Platform Software Engineer 2 on the Spectra Batch team, building systems that run batch jobs and workflows at scale. My work spans backend microservices, workflow infrastructure, CI/CD automation, observability, and platform reliability.

Outside work, I love watching and playing football. Since school, I’ve somehow managed to pick up an injury almost every year—a hairline fracture in the wrist, a ligament tear, or, when everything else is fine, an ankle sprain. I like to joke that it comes with being an attacker, though I’d happily skip that tradition.

I’m regular at the gym and also love watching UFC. Learning the guitar, meanwhile, remains a recurring side quest: I pick it up, learn a few chords, and then life’s other chores take over. One of these days, I’ll make it past the opening act.
